# Botanical Study Android concept

Status: dashboard direction and Botanical Study display name approved by the
maintainer; matching Theme Gallery surface awaiting review in GitHub issue
#115.

This concept is grounded in direct inspection of digitized Smithsonian
herbarium sheets, public-domain botanical plates in the Biodiversity Heritage
Library, Kew's presentation of botanical illustration as scientific study,
and the approved Botanical Study semantic palette. It is a concept reference,
not production UI, a golden, or raster-frame source art.

## Visual evidence translated

- Herbarium sheets contribute warm paper fields, disciplined specimen mounting,
  accession labels, measurement ticks, and quiet olive-sage coloration.
- Botanical plates contribute precise line work, spacious ivory composition,
  small anatomy-study details, and restrained specimen color.
- Contemporary exhibition presentation contributes clean typography, generous
  whitespace, and the separation of study material from explanatory content.
- The concept translates those traits into original Clinical Calendar chrome.
  It does not reproduce a specimen, institutional logo, label, or plate layout.

## Review surface

- `calendar-dashboard-concept-v1.png` preserves the Android-tablet Calendar
  regions while using a warm ivory and pale sage nine-slice housing.
- `theme-gallery-concept-v1.png` carries the approved identity into Settings,
  uses the dashboard as its thumbnail, presents the five semantic swatches,
  and makes Botanical Study a selected Preview candidate without applying it.
- Opaque content bays keep decorative line art outside live information.
- Accession tabs, measurement rules, mounting-strip geometry, and botanical
  corner studies make the identity specific without becoming floral wallpaper.
- Icons, rails, outlines, patterns, and complete text labels keep calendar and
  progress semantics redundant rather than color-only.

## Final name

**Botanical Study** is approved. The name accurately describes the research
desk and scientific-illustration character, stays contemporary and
professional, and avoids the antique or decorative implications of names such
as Herbarium, Garden Journal, or Botanical Archive.

## Approval boundary

Approve the direction only if the dashboard establishes:

- a recognizable scientific botanical-study identity without floral wallpaper,
  cottagecore, apothecary nostalgia, or institutional imitation;
- warm ivory paper, sage housing, aubergine rules, dusty rose work semantics,
  and pale orchid protected-day semantics;
- restrained specimen labels, measurement ticks, and line art confined to
  inactive outer chrome;
- calm, fully opaque content bays with no plant imagery behind live data;
- unchanged Calendar regions, controls, navigation, and domain language;
- complete visible labels with wrapping instead of ellipses or truncation; and
- visible CLINICAL, WORK, PROTECTED, TODAY, selected-date, attention, and
  progress cues.

The exact token values and semantic assignments remain authoritative in
`docs/research/themes/botanical-study-palette.md`. Image-generation text and
miniature details are illustrative and must not be treated as implementation
copy or color measurements.

Production Flutter, final nine-slice artwork, implementation goldens, Theme
gallery concept, and physical Android acceptance remain outside this approval
step.
