# Heritage Field Notes Android concept

Status: Calendar dashboard direction and **Field Archive** display name approved
by the maintainer in GitHub issue #118.

Issue #118 asks for approval of the Heritage Field Notes Android tablet
concept and its final display name. This artifact is a concept reference, not
production UI, a golden, or nine-slice source art.

## Recommendation

Use **Field Archive** as the final display name. It preserves the scientific
field-record origin while describing the ordered, accountable system shown in
the concept. The shorter name also avoids suggesting a handwriting-heavy
notebook simulation.

## Research translation

- The [Smithsonian Field Book Project](https://siarchives.si.edu/about/field-book-project) describes field books as original records of scientific discovery whose value comes from dates, localities, observations, and specimen context. The concept translates that structure into ordered calendar cells, record-like event chips, and indexed navigation.
- The [Library of Congress guidance for works on paper](https://www.loc.gov/preservation/care/paper.html) emphasizes clean work areas, flat storage, and supportive protective enclosures such as archival folders and document boxes. The concept uses flat opaque content bays inside a protective book-board housing instead of distressed paper effects.
- The [National Park Service Museum Handbook](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/museums/museumhandbook.htm) centers documentation, accountability, accessioning, cataloging, and storage. Numbered brass tabs, registration marks, and disciplined rules reference that catalog system without copying institutional marks.

## Visual system

- Warm stone and parchment content bays remain opaque and texture-free.
- Forest ink anchors navigation and structural rules.
- Walnut book-board rails create a durable housing without simulated wood grain or leather.
- Muted brass tabs and registration details provide archival indexing.
- Oxblood, blue-gray, sage, and teal remain controlled semantic accents.
- Handwriting, stains, torn edges, page curls, maps, camouflage, tactical webbing, and military insignia are excluded.
