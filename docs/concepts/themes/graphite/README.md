# Graphite Android and verification concepts

Status: dashboard and signed-out verification directions awaiting maintainer
review in GitHub issue #117.

These concepts are grounded first in the repository's launcher master and
Graphite semantic palette, then in direct research on graphite material
properties and contemporary precision-instrument design. They are concept
references, not production UI, goldens, or raster-frame source art.

## Visual evidence translated

- The launcher master contributes near-black graphite, layered charcoal, cool
  silver structural arcs, a recessed square grid, rounded precision edges, and
  one restrained emerald indicator.
- USGS material evidence supports gray-black, opaque surfaces with controlled
  metallic luster rather than glossy black glass.
- Precision-display design research contributes pure geometry, matte reflection
  control, thin aluminum structure, and form that makes function visible.
- The concepts translate those traits into original Clinical Calendar chrome.
  They do not enlarge or trace the launcher mark into panel geometry.

## Review surfaces

- `calendar-dashboard-concept-v1.png` preserves the Android-tablet Calendar
  regions inside a monolithic graphite chassis with flat opaque content bays.
- `verification-flow-concept-v1.png` presents the sequential email-entry and
  one-time-code states side by side for review. Both states use the fixed
  signed-out Graphite identity and do not load a Student theme.
- Cobalt, violet, and brass remain domain accents. Teal is the only ambient
  signal, and coral is reserved for errors and urgent attention.
- Icons, rails, outlines, patterns, and complete text labels keep calendar and
  progress semantics redundant rather than color-only.

## Approval boundary

Approve the direction only if the concepts establish:

- a neutral precision-instrument identity aligned with the launcher without
  reproducing its logo as panel structure;
- matte graphite slabs, cool-silver edges, recessed square-grid details, and
  restrained emerald indicators;
- a clear separation from Federation 2399's burgundy understructure, sweeping
  pale hull lips, ambient plum, and spacecraft-console character;
- opaque content bays with no metallic texture behind live data or fields;
- unchanged Calendar regions, controls, navigation, and domain language;
- complete visible labels with wrapping instead of ellipses or truncation;
- visible CLINICAL, WORK, PROTECTED, TODAY, selected-date, attention, and
  progress cues; and
- a coherent signed-out email and code flow that styles only the application,
  never the delivered email message.

The exact token values and semantic assignments remain authoritative in
`docs/research/themes/graphite-palette.md`. Image-generation text and miniature
details are illustrative and must not be treated as implementation copy or
color measurements.

Production Flutter, final nine-slice artwork, implementation goldens, Theme
Gallery concept, and physical Android acceptance remain outside this approval
step.
