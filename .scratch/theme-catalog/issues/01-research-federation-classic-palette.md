## Question

Which primary visual and production references support a recognizable TNG-era LCARS palette and shape language, and how should that evidence become a complete, original Clinical Calendar semantic palette with contrast-safe standard and Enhanced accessibility mappings?

## Answer

First-party Star Trek material supports broad rounded LCARS structure, warm and lavender high-luminance blocks on a dark field, and internal visual consistency. The completed brief converts that evidence into an original semantic token system, verified contrast table, redundant calendar/progress cues, Enhanced accessibility overrides, nine-slice direction, and prohibited uses without copying production screens or claiming canonical hex values.
