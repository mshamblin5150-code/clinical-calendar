# Research the Heritage Field Notes Semantic Palette

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

Which high-trust primary color, archival-material, and accessibility sources support a durable professional field-journal direction, and how should warm stone/parchment, forest green, walnut, muted brass, and oxblood candidates map to every Clinical Calendar semantic state?

## Answer

Treat paper, parchment, leather, metal, and archival housings as controlled
material references—not as distressed faux realism. The proposed light
semantic system uses parchment/warm-stone surfaces, forest for principal and
Clinical Session roles, oxblood for Work Shift, brass for Protected Day and
Unscheduled Hours, and a restrained blue-gray archival ink for Scheduled Hours
and information. It includes complete component/domain tokens, verified
contrast, redundant calendar/progress cues, deterministic Enhanced
accessibility overrides, and explicit anti-rustic/anti-military drift rules.
The display name remains provisional until Android concept approval.
