## Question

Which high-trust primary color, material, and accessibility sources support a clean professional coastal direction without literal beach imagery, and how should mist blue, sea-glass teal, warm sand, shell white, and controlled coral candidates map to every Clinical Calendar semantic state?

## Answer

Adopt the accessibility-calibrated light palette documented in [Coastal Calm semantic palette research](../../../docs/research/themes/coastal-calm-palette.md): shell-white/mist surfaces, sea-glass teal Clinical identity, clear blue Work Shift identity, warm-sand Protected Day treatment, and coral reserved for urgent semantics and focus. The note supplies the full token table, measured contrast pairs, calendar/progress/synchronization mappings, optional Enhanced accessibility overrides, source rationale, and prohibited pairings. All hexes are original design inferences, with coastal motifs confined to housing/noninteractive chrome.
