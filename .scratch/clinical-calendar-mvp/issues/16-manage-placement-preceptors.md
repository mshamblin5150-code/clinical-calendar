# Manage Primary and Additional Preceptors

Type: task
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Parent issue

Reported during QA of ticket 07.

## What's wrong

The Student can see a Preceptor breakdown but cannot add, edit, remove, or designate the Primary Preceptor for a Clinical Placement.

## What I expected

Clinical Placement management supports reusable Preceptors, exactly one Primary Preceptor, additional Preceptors, and reassignment without resetting placement progress or history.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open a Clinical Placement.
2. View its Preceptor breakdown.
3. Observe that no management actions are available.

## Blocked by

None - can start immediately.

## Answer

Clinical Placement management supports placement and Target Hours editing, adding placements and Preceptors, editing names, and assigning exactly one Primary Preceptor while preserving history.
