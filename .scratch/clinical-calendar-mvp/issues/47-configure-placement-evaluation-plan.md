# Configure the Placement Evaluation Plan

Type: task
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## What's wrong

Clinical Placement management exposes Interim Review cadence but does not show or configure the required beginning/end evaluations. Initial Self-Evaluation, Final Self-Evaluation, and Final Clinical Placement Review exist only implicitly in the evaluation list.

## What I expected

Each Clinical Placement has an explicit Evaluation Plan in Settings containing the Interim Review cadence and Required/Not required controls for Initial Self-Evaluation, Final Self-Evaluation, and Final Clinical Placement Review. The selected placement's evaluation list respects those settings.

## Answer

Clinical Placement management now groups Interim Review cadence, Initial Self-Evaluation, Final Self-Evaluation, and Final Clinical Placement Review under an explicit Evaluation Plan. Every placement and newly added placement defaults all three boundary evaluations to Required. Rendered QA changed Final Clinical Placement Review to Not required and confirmed it disappeared from Family Medicine's checklist while Initial and Final Self-Evaluations remained.
