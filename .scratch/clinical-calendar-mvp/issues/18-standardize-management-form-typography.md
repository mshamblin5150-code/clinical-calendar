# Standardize Management Form Typography

Type: task
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Parent issue

Reported during QA of ticket 07.

## What's wrong

Clinical Placement management and Student profile use inconsistent text sizing between field labels, values, and Preceptor controls.

## What I expected

Equivalent labels, inputs, selects, and actions use a consistent readable type scale across every management form.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open Clinical Placement management or Student profile.
2. Compare Placement name and Target Hours with Preceptor name controls.
3. Observe the inconsistent type sizes.

## Blocked by

None - can start immediately.

## Answer

Equivalent form labels now use a consistent 13 px scale and editable values use 14 px across Clinical Placement management, Student profile, Settings, templates, and commitment editing.
