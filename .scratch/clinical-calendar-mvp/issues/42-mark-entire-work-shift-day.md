# Mark the Entire Work Shift Day

Type: task
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## What's wrong

Work Shifts have a Borg-themed event card or compact dot, but the containing calendar day still looks like an ordinary open day. Protected Days have a stronger full-cell treatment.

## What I expected

Any day containing a Work Shift receives a restrained gunmetal/teal full-cell treatment in addition to its visible Work Shift card or dot. It remains visually distinct from the diagonal graphite/silver Protected Day treatment and does not rely on color alone.

## Answer

Work Shift days now carry a full-cell gunmetal/teal wash and inset machinery rail while retaining the labeled event card on desktop and Work Shift dot/accessibility label on phone. The treatment remains distinct from Protected Days' diagonal graphite/silver pattern. Desktop and 390 px phone checks confirmed the styling and zero horizontal overflow.
