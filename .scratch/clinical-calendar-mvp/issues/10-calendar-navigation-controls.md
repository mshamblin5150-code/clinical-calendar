# Make Calendar Navigation Controls Functional

Type: task
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Parent issue

Reported during QA of ticket 07.

## What's wrong

Previous and next controls do not change the displayed calendar period, and calendar view controls do not provide a complete navigable experience.

## What I expected

Previous and next change the displayed period consistently in Month, Week, and Agenda views, with the current period clearly identified.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open the calendar.
2. Select previous or next beside the current period.
3. Observe that the period does not change.

## Blocked by

None - can start immediately.

## Answer

Previous and next now change the displayed period, and Month, Week, and Agenda render distinct schedule views from the same state.
